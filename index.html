Speeds are ground speed from aircraft ADS-B broadcasts, picked up by volunteer receivers and shared through the free adsb.lol and adsb.fi feeds. The app scans busy airspace on rotation and re-checks the leader and its closest challengers every 10 seconds. Aircraft over open ocean, outside receiver range, or flying without a public transponder signal (most military jets) can't be seen, so "fastest" means fastest among aircraft that are visible right now. Readings above 680 kt need a second matching report before they count, which filters out data glitches.
